# Recipe Editorial Standard

## Record and review state

Every generated recipe must begin as `editorialStatus: draft`, `testCookStatus: required`, `foodSafetyReview: required`, and `koreanLanguageReview: required`. It may not be presented as published or tested without the corresponding human evidence.

Use exactly one schema and template. Measurements should include metric weights/volumes and practical U.S. customary equivalents when sensible. Times are estimates; temperature and observable doneness cues control safety. Nutrition remains `not-calculated` unless a documented calculation workflow is added later.

## Integrity rules

- Do not invent origin stories, biographies, test results, awards, testimonials, ratings, review counts, dates, media, or personal anecdotes.
- Use “traditional,” “authentic,” “royal,” “street food,” or “restaurant-style” only when supported and reviewed.
- Label contemporary adaptations directly.
- Add Hangul and romanization only when reasonably certain; keep language review required until approval.
- Preserve the locked inventory, IDs, categories, and counts. Title improvement is allowed only before Phase 2 URL lock.
- Keep instructions complete and unique. Every step includes action, time estimate, temperature when relevant, cue, adjacent safety note, and media reference.

## Allergens and substitutions

Flag soy, wheat/gluten, sesame, fish, shellfish, egg, dairy, peanuts, tree nuts, and alcohol. Substitutions never promise an allergen-free result. Every record warns readers to check labels and manage cross-contact.

## Meat and seafood completeness

All 20 grilled-meat records require the integrated marinade/seasoning contract in the schema. All 15 seafood records require buying, thawing, cleaning/shell prep, drying, scoring/skewering where relevant, surface prep, sticking prevention, time estimate, doneness cues, applicable temperature, shell discard guidance, and cross-contact notes.
