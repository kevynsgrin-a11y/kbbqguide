# 90-Day Growth and Revenue Plan

Checkpoint date: 2026-07-14

Status: draft operating plan only. All 80 recipes and every supporting asset remain unpublished until their human gates pass. Calendar dates are planning coordinates in `America/Los_Angeles`, not publication claims or authorization.

## Channel roles

- Website: canonical source, search destination, conversion surface, and durable archive.
- Email: consented owned relationship, weekly menu, launches, and retention.
- YouTube long-form: durable demonstrations and authority after real production.
- Shorts/Reels/TikTok: native technique discovery with channel-adapted hooks and no unsupported shortcuts.
- Instagram: visual education, Stories, community, and transparent comment-to-DM entry.
- Pinterest: original evergreen menu, checklist, and recipe concepts.
- Facebook: shareable recipes, events, Lives, and discussions.
- Optional communities: helpful manual participation under each community’s link and disclosure rules; no automated spam.

## Flagship atomization package

For every approved flagship recipe, plan one canonical recipe page, one 6–10 minute video outline, three distinct short scripts, one Instagram carousel, two to four original pin concepts, five Story frames, one Facebook post, one email module, one FAQ/technique snippet, one disclosed shoppable module, and one photo/video shot list. Preserve facts while adapting hook, length, CTA, aspect ratio, and link behavior; do not duplicate identical captions.

## Weekly operating rhythm

Monday flagship recipe/carousel/pins/teaser; Tuesday technique short/poll/community response; Wednesday second recipe/Facebook/Q&A/internal links; Thursday long-form/supporting guide/native short/pins; Friday third recipe/weekend email/disclosed shopping module; Saturday fourth recipe and approved live/community activity; Sunday optional refresh plus analytics, comments, errors, conversions, opt-outs, broken-link review, and next-week planning.

Content mix target: 35% recipes/technique, 25% hosting/menu/setup/cleanup, 15% banchan/fresh balance, 15% disclosed shopping/gear, and 10% culture/terminology/community/permissioned UGC.

## Days 1–15

Resolve brand/domain/disclosure placeholders; complete human review for the intended 28 launch recipes; configure approved privacy, consent, email, social, analytics, and search accounts; finish the free planner and welcome sequence; prepare three transparent shopping bundles; apply to selected affiliate programs with real site details; and preserve zero fabricated/broken links.

## Days 16–30

Only after approval, soft-launch the reviewed set and at least four guides; begin the weekly schedule; test opt-in placement and disclosed modules; publish genuinely useful buyer-intent guidance; collect real questions with consent; create the class waitlist; and retain the clean UX baseline with ads disabled.

## Days 31–60

Continue four reviewed recipes per week; refine pages from observed search and engagement rather than invented demand; test the free planner and welcome flow; run a reviewed live class pilot; document real product comparisons; evaluate—but do not automatically enable—ad and commerce providers.

## Days 61–90

Complete the planned reviewed release sequence; prepare five to eight evidence-backed shopping/comparison guides; optimize the top pages from real data; evaluate a second digital product or replay; and prepare a sponsor offering with explicit deliverables, rights, disclosures, and editorial independence.

## Success and stop conditions

Track approval throughput, broken links, consent errors, opt-outs, complaints, page/task completion, planner use, email engagement, disclosed module use, and conversion only after lawful collection begins. Stop a campaign or integration on missing approval, safety/cultural defect, broken consent, unknown merchant permission, deceptive disclosure, exposed secret, invalid link, or high-severity accessibility/performance regression.
