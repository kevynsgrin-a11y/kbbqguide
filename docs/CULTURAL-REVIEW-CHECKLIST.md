# Cultural and Korean-Language Review Checklist

- [ ] Korean name, Hangul, spacing, romanization, and English title are accurate and consistent.
- [ ] Category and preparation terms describe the dish without forcing it into an inaccurate “Korean BBQ” frame.
- [ ] Any historical, regional, royal-court, restaurant, street-food, or “traditional/authentic” claim has a traceable source and appropriate nuance.
- [ ] Contemporary adaptation is labeled and does not displace a classic preparation without explanation.
- [ ] Ingredient substitutions are framed as availability adaptations, not equivalent cultural claims.
- [ ] Introductory context avoids exoticizing, flattening regional diversity, or presenting one household method as universal.
- [ ] Serving and etiquette guidance distinguishes common practice from a mandatory rule.
- [ ] Korean-language search terms are not added solely for keyword stuffing.
- [ ] Source notes separate documented facts, editorial judgment, and unresolved questions.
- [ ] Reviewer identity, role/qualification, date, changes, and approval are logged.

Until every item is approved, `koreanLanguageReview` remains `required`.
