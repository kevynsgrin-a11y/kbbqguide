## Provider interface

The future adapter accepts an approved template ID, consented subscriber reference, allowlisted interest tags, approved on-site URLs, campaign ID, and suppression status. It must reject raw email addresses in URLs/analytics, missing consent, missing postal address, missing unsubscribe controls, unapproved affiliate placement, and send requests without human marketing approval.

## Retention and requests

Before collection begins, document the provider, purpose, lawful basis, fields, retention, access/deletion workflow, suppression-list treatment, breach escalation, and export path. Suppression records must be retained only as necessary to honor opt-out obligations and protected from campaign use.
