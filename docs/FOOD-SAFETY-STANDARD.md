# Food and Home-Grilling Safety Standard

This is an editorial baseline, not individualized medical advice. A qualified human must review every recipe and all safety-facing guides before production.

## Indoor appliance rule

Never recommend charcoal, propane, a butane camping stove, or an outdoor grill indoors. CPSC warns that charcoal must not be used indoors even with ventilation because carbon monoxide can reach lethal levels. Indoor content may show only an appliance whose manufacturer explicitly approves indoor use, following its clearance, surface, circuit, ventilation, and supervision instructions. A working carbon-monoxide alarm does not make a prohibited appliance safe indoors.

## Handling controls

- Wash hands; sanitize work surfaces; separate ready-to-eat foods from raw meat and seafood.
- Do not rinse raw poultry. Keep separate raw and cooked plates, tongs, brushes, and cutting boards.
- Thaw and marinate under refrigeration. Reserve serving sauce before raw-food contact.
- Discard marinade that contacted raw meat, poultry, or seafood unless a reviewed instruction explicitly brings it to a safe boil and controls cross-contact; the safer default is discard.
- Keep warnings adjacent to their relevant step. No ad, disclosure, accordion, or unrelated module may separate them.
- Use a calibrated food thermometer. Time and grill marks are never the only doneness test.

## Minimum internal temperatures

| Food                                             | Minimum                           | Additional rule                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Whole beef, pork, veal, lamb steaks/chops/roasts | 145°F / 63°C                      | Rest at least 3 minutes                                                                                                                           |
| Ground meats                                     | 160°F / 71°C                      | Measure in the thickest center                                                                                                                    |
| Poultry, including ground poultry                | 165°F / 74°C                      | Measure the thickest part, avoiding bone                                                                                                          |
| Fin fish                                         | 145°F / 63°C                      | Also record visual doneness cues                                                                                                                  |
| Shellfish                                        | Recipe-specific reviewed guidance | Use applicable temperature plus opacity/firmness or shell-opening cues; discard shells that do not open when the reviewed method requires opening |

## Higher-risk readers

Pregnant, immunocompromised, older, and very young readers require explicit cautions where raw or undercooked egg, meat, seafood, unpasteurized ingredients, or alcohol is relevant. “Alcohol cooks off” is not an automatic claim; the recipe must give an appropriate alternative or reviewed caution.
