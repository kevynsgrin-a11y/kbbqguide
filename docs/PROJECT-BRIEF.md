# Project Brief

## Promise

`KBBQGuide` will be a premium, static-first guide that helps home cooks plan, shop for, safely cook, serve, and clean up after Korean BBQ at home.

## Audience and primary journey

The primary audience is an English-speaking U.S. home cook or host who wants a credible Korean BBQ meal without restaurant equipment. The core journey is `start-here guide → menu size → shopping list → prep timeline → recipes → safe cook and serve → cleanup and leftovers`. Repeat use comes from menus, scaling, shopping lists, seasonal recipes, hosting tools, and make-ahead plans.

## Product classification

Hybrid content platform and browser-side planning utility. Core reading and tools remain free and usable without registration.

## First credible release

- Repository contains exactly 80 reviewed recipe records using one schema and one page template.
- Public launch contains 28 recipes that have passed editorial, test-kitchen, food-safety, and Korean-language review.
- At least four reviewed guides and the planner, menu builder, shopping list, and prep timeline are usable.
- Broken internal links, duplicate canonicals, invalid Recipe JSON-LD, active unapproved affiliate links, and critical/high defects are all zero.
- Monetization remains restrained and disabled until disclosure, consent, UX, and performance gates pass.

## Non-goals for the initial release

- No user accounts, social network, arbitrary user HTML, live merchant pricing, nutrition claims, public ratings, automatic production publishing, SMS launch, decorative WebGL dependency, or unreviewed generative publishing.
- No charcoal, propane, butane camping stove, or outdoor grill recommendation for indoor use.
- No invented brand identity, legal value, address, contact detail, analytics ID, affiliate destination, claim, review, date, image, video, or test result.

## Constraints and placeholders

All supplied `{{PLACEHOLDER}}` values remain literal until human replacement. Provider integrations are interfaces only; secrets must remain server-side. Media stays manifest-driven and placeholder-only until rights and subject accuracy are approved.

## Success measures

The working 90-day measures are the specification's operational criteria, not guarantees. Quality gates take precedence over publishing volume or revenue targets.
