# Advertising Placement Policy

Checkpoint date: 2026-07-14

All four reserved slots are disabled. No ad network, script, iframe, request, auction, identifier, consent signal, or revenue impression exists in Phase 6.

## Reserved locations

- after a page introduction;
- after a completed recipe card or full instructions;
- optional desktop rail;
- optional closeable mobile anchor after usability testing.

## Prohibited locations and behavior

- inside ingredients or equipment lists;
- between a safety warning and its related instruction;
- inside step cues, allergen notes, seafood handling, doneness, storage, or reheating guidance;
- above the primary page purpose or close enough to a control to invite accidental clicks;
- autoplay with sound, forced interstitials, deceptive system-style UI, or content-shifting late loads;
- any placement that blocks keyboard, screen-reader, print, reduced-motion, or no-JavaScript use.

## Activation gate

Activation requires a real provider contract, privacy and consent review, performance budget, cumulative-layout-shift test, accessibility test, close-control test, mobile viewport test, exclusion-zone validation across all 80 recipes, and written approval. Ads stay disabled if content, UX, consent, or performance evidence is incomplete.
